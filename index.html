How long does editing take?

Standard orders deliver within 24h. Large batches or complex lifestyle work get a clear timeline up front.

How much per image?

Starter from $1.5/image, Standard from $1.0/image (10+), Monthly from $149/mo. Final price varies by category, volume and complexity.

What payment methods?

Flat USD pricing, paid via PayPal, Wise, Stripe or USDT — avoiding local-payment friction across markets.

Which platforms do you support?

Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Etsy, Wish, Walmart and major regional platforms — output to each one's main/detail image spec.

Any requirements for the source photo?

Phone or flat-lay shots are fine, the clearer the better. Leave background, lighting and retouching to us.

Will my product photos leak?

We respect seller confidentiality: source files are deleted on request after delivery and never reused publicly without permission.
